`00-all-four-clips-v1.mp4` = the four clips concatenated (99 s). Source: `deck_v1.py` (re-render: `python -m manim -qh deck_v1.py S1_Pipeline S2_Collapse S3_RateAware S4_Hull`, needs manim + scipy, reads analysis/rd/seven-scene-points-v2.csv).

| 3 | 03-rate-aware-growth-v1.mp4 (frames 03a, 03b, 03c) | 31 s | the new route: rule `split t ⟺ ΔD_t / ΔR_t > λ` (ΔD = rendered error mass on triangle t, ΔR = bytes of the new vertex, λ = price). Then 7-scene bars: λ 2e-3 → 19.6 MB / −0.26 dB / 36× mean; λ 6e-3 → 8.8 MB / −0.88 dB / 80× mean. | "Rate enters the growth loop. λ is the only knob; the vertex count is an output and differs per scene (41–100 % of dense at 2e-3, 15–43 % at 6e-3). | 4 | 04-hull-old-vs-new-v1.mp4 (frames 04a, 04b) | 27 s | Room: 49 old-route configs (K×R × collapse level) with their hull, then the λ points and the new hull (both hulls drawn as smooth monotone curves through the measured hull vertices). Then 7 scenes relative to each original: thin old-route hulls per scene, λ pairs, mean diamonds 23.6 MB/−0.44 (old default), 19.6/−0.26, 8.8/−0.88. | "Same codec, same scenes; the only change is when the vertices are chosen. Smoothing: hull vertices are the measured upper-concave-envelope points; the drawn curve is a monotone PCHIP interpolant through them in log-MB (passes through every hull point, no overshoot). Old-route per-scene hulls use only the level-labelled collapse points (labels starting with "v"), clipped to ≥ 4.5 MB and ≥ −1.5 dB.
