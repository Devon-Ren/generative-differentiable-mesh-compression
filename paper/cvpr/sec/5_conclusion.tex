\section{Conclusion}
Opaque differentiable meshes cannot be shrunk after training, so \BM decides their size while they grow: a growth rule priced in encoded bytes, a controller that turns a file size into that price with the encoder in the loop, and a codec whose decoder writes a standard mesh. The result is a file of the requested size, within about 10\% on four datasets, 31--122$\times$ smaller than the trained model at 0.3--1.3\dB, in one training run. The remaining gap to Gaussian codecs is the representation's; closing it, and learning the entropy model that the byte probe now measures, are the next steps.
